# Quantification over Structure

### An Entropy-Driven, Self-Iterative Architecture for LLMs

**E³ is an open research initiative exploring how AI agents can discover transferable decision structures, regulate their own learning, and preserve validated capabilities through experience.**

## Research Vision

Rather than relying exclusively on predefined human knowledge structures, E³ investigates whether AI can discover **quantifiable relationships that remain useful across tasks, representations, and domains**.

The central research question is:

> Can AI agents discover minimal decision-sufficient structures from diverse task experiences and use them to improve generalization?

## Core Architecture

E³ proposes three interconnected mechanisms:

* **Logical State Stack (LSS):** Tracks reasoning states, dependencies, constraints, and unresolved obligations.
* **Cumulative Tension (CT):** Accumulates unresolved prediction errors and structural discrepancies to guide intervention.
* **Protective Value (PV):** Evaluates the preservation of validated capabilities during learning and updates.

The framework further explores:

* **AHP-DSA:** Quantitative evaluation of candidate decision-sufficient structures.
* **TCA:** Topology-Constrained Agent supervision for structural discovery in multi-agent learning.
* **Obligation-Aware Generation:** Dynamic tracking of structural commitments during language generation.

## Research Direction

We aim to investigate whether these mechanisms can support more generalizable, efficient, and reliable AI agents through formal modeling and reproducible experiments.

The project is currently developing its theoretical framework and experimental program.
